Review demonstration

Android recording

A demonstration of the user-started microphone foreground service: open SnoreSpot, start recording, switch to Home, view the ongoing recording notification, return, finish and open the report.

Demonstration setup

Recorded on October 15, 2026 using an isolated Android 16 / API 36 test emulator, actual SnoreSpot Flutter screens, and native microphone capture and on-device classification. Microphone and notification permissions were already granted. There is no account login, owner recording or purchase information in this footage.

The QA build uses 30-second segments to demonstrate incremental analysis in a short video. The production candidate uses 120-second segments. This is a functional demonstration, not footage of a Google Play purchase or a physical-device acceptance test.

The microphone service is started only after the user opens the app and starts recording. A reminder asks the user to open the app; it does not start hidden recording at boot or at an unattended scheduled time.

SnoreSpot is a recording and personal observation tool, not a medical device. It cannot diagnose, treat, cure or prevent illness. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about medical symptoms, diagnosis or treatment.
