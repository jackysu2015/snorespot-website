English

How do I start recording?

Place your iPhone on a stable, ventilated surface near the bed with the microphone unobstructed, then start a recording in SnoreSpot. Recording continues after you lock the screen or switch apps; return to the app to finish and save it. Long overnight reliability is still being validated before public release.

How do I review possible snoring?

Open a saved night and start local analysis on a supported iPhone. Keep the app open while analysis runs. Listen to possible sound clips and confirm or correct labels yourself. Detection may be wrong; finding no clips does not prove there was no snoring. SnoreSpot is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea.

What is free, and what does the permanent unlock add?

When purchasing is enabled, three different nights with successfully completed analysis are free. Reanalyzing the same night, cancelling or an unsuccessful analysis does not use another night. A single non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks analysis of additional nights, without a subscription. Existing recordings and results remain viewable. The App Store shows the price for your region.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases on the unlock screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Connect to the App Store while restoring. An Apple purchase does not promise transfer to Android or another store, and Family Sharing is not offered for this unlock.

Where is my data, and how can I delete it?

Your recordings and journal stay in the app's local storage; SnoreSpot does not automatically upload them. Delete a recording in the app to remove its local audio. Uninstalling may remove local data. Exports, recipient apps and device backups are managed separately. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

Email info@snorespot.app. Include your iPhone model, iOS and app version, and a brief description of the issue. Please do not send a recording unless it is necessary and you have checked it for other people's voices.

简体中文

如何开始录音？

将 iPhone 放在床边稳定、通风的位置，确保麦克风无遮挡，然后在 SnoreSpot 中主动开始录音。锁屏或切换应用后录音仍会继续；返回应用结束并保存。公开发布前，整夜可靠性仍需完成真机验证。

如何回听疑似鼾声？

打开已保存的夜晚，在受支持的 iPhone 上开始本机分析。分析时请保持应用打开。回听疑似片段，并自行确认或纠正标签。识别可能出错；未找到片段也不代表没有鼾声。SnoreSpot 不是医疗器械，不诊断睡眠呼吸暂停。

免费额度和永久解锁是什么？

购买功能开启后，三个不同夜晚的成功分析免费。同一夜晚重跑、取消或分析失败不会消耗新的免费夜晚。一次性非消耗型内购可永久解锁更多夜晚的分析，没有订阅。已有录音和结果仍可查看。价格以你所在地区的 App Store 为准。

如何恢复购买？

在解锁页面使用“恢复购买”，并确保登录购买时使用的 Apple 账号、可以连接 App Store。Apple 购买不承诺转移到 Android 或其他商店；此解锁不提供家庭共享。

数据保存在哪里，如何删除？

录音和日记保存在应用本地，SnoreSpot 不会自动上传。在应用中删除录音可移除其本地音频。卸载可能移除本地数据；已导出的副本、接收应用和设备备份需分别管理。详见隐私说明。

联系我们

邮件：info@snorespot.app。请说明 iPhone 型号、iOS 和应用版本，以及遇到的问题。非必要请勿发送录音；如需发送，请先检查是否包含他人声音。
